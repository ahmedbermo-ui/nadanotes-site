NadaNotes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

NadaNotes is a note-taking app that works entirely on your device. It has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. Your notes, voice memos, photos, scanned documents and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, and the app does not use the internet to work. The only exceptions are the Google Drive backup and Android backup described below, which happen only if you turn them on.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to record voice memos, only when you tap the record button. A notification is shown while recording.
  • Camera: to take a photo or scan a document into a note, only when you choose to.
  • Notifications: to show that a recording is in progress, and for backup reminders if you turn them on.
  • Biometrics: to unlock locked notes with your fingerprint or face, if you turn the lock on. Biometric data stays with your phone's system; the app never sees it.
  • Storage (older Android versions only): to save automatic backups to the Documents folder on your device.

Backups

Backups are files that you create, on your device. You decide where they go (for example email, Google Drive or a computer). If you choose a backup password, the backup is encrypted and cannot be opened without it — not even by us. We never receive your backups.

Google Drive backup (optional). If you turn on "Back up to Google Drive", you choose a Google account and allow NadaNotes to use its own backup folder in that account's Google Drive. The app then uploads its backup file (your notes, voice memos, photos and scans, encrypted with your backup password if you set one) directly from your phone to your Drive, daily or weekly, and keeps the three newest. By default the files go to Drive's hidden app folder, which only NadaNotes can read; you can also choose to keep a visible copy in a "NadaNotes backups" folder. The app asks only for access to files it creates itself; it cannot see anything else in your Drive. The backups go straight to Google and never pass through us; we cannot access them. Google stores them under Google's Privacy Policy and they count toward your Drive storage. To stop, tap "Stop Drive backup" in the app; to delete the backups, remove NadaNotes under Drive › Settings › Manage apps (hidden folder) or delete the "NadaNotes backups" folder. NadaNotes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Android backup. If Google backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android copies the app's notes database and settings (text notes, lists, tables, titles and transcripts) to your own Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone or reinstall the app. Voice memos, photos and scanned documents are not included. This is a feature of Android run by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; on recent Android versions the backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock. We do not receive or have access to it. You can turn it off at any time in your phone settings (Settings › Google › Backup).

Sharing

When you share a note or send it by email, the app hands it to the app you pick (such as your email app). We are not involved and receive nothing.

Children

NadaNotes is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the contact email shown on the NadaNotes listing in Google Play.

سياسة الخصوصية — ملخص

NadaNotes يعمل بالكامل على جهازك: بلا حساب، بلا خادم، بلا إعلانات، وبلا تحليلات. لا نجمع أي بيانات. ملاحظاتك وتسجيلاتك وصورك تبقى على هاتفك ولا تُرسل إلينا أبدًا. النسخ الاحتياطية ملفات تنشئها أنت وتحفظها حيث تشاء، ويمكن تشفيرها بكلمة مرور لا نعرفها. وإذا فعّلت النسخ إلى Google Drive، يرفع التطبيق ملف النسخة (الملاحظات والمذكرات الصوتية والصور، مشفّرًا إن وضعت كلمة مرور) مباشرة من هاتفك إلى Drive الخاص بك، ولا يمر بنا ولا نستطيع الوصول إليه. وإذا فعّلت نسخ أندرويد الاحتياطي في إعدادات هاتفك، يحفظ أندرويد ملاحظاتك النصية وقوائمك وإعداداتك في حسابك أنت على Google (دون المذكرات الصوتية والصور)، ولا نستطيع نحن الوصول إليها.
